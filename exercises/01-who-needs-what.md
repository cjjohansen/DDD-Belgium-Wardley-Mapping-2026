# Exercise 1: Who needs what?

**Canvas steps:** 1 to 4 (purpose, scope, users, user needs)
**Time:** about 15 minutes
**After:** the cup of tea and the Wardley Mapping Canvas

## The scenario

Harbour Insurance insures ships, cargo and the businesses around the port of Antwerp. It has about 2,000 employees and 300,000 policies, 6 product teams, about 40 developers and one architect.

Every team now has AI coding agents. Code arrives in hours, not days. Yet customers get new features no faster than before.

Management asks: "We pay for AI coding agents, but we don't see faster delivery. What is happening?"

## Your task

1. Write the purpose: why does this organisation exist? For example, get new insurance products and changes to customers quickly and safely.
2. Write the scope: what is in, and what is out? For example, from a product hypothesis to customers using the change.
3. Name the users. Who uses or depends on the thing you are mapping?
4. Under each user, write their needs on sticky notes, in their own words.

Stop there. The value chain comes in Exercise 2.

## Domain expert brief

The domain expert in your group can answer questions like these.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| How long does a pull request wait for review? | Before AI: less than a day. Now: about 3 days |
| Who reviews? | Two senior developers, and the architect reviews about 70% of pull requests |
| How big are the pull requests? | Bigger than before, because the AI writes more code |
| How many pull requests per week? | About 3 times more than before |
| Do people pair program? | Rarely. "It's slower" |
| Where does the AI tool come from? | Bought as a product, paid per seat |

## Back together

- How many different users did you find?
- Which user has the most needs, and which the fewest?
- Are any needs shared by several users?

## Before Exercise 2: pick your anchor

To build one map, you need one anchor. Ask what question the map answers. For Harbour Insurance it is "Why is delivery slow?" Pick the user whose need matters most for that question, for example Management with the need "Time to market". The other needs become components in your chain.
