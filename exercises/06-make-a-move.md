# Exercise 6: Make a Move

**Time:** 15 minutes  
**After:** EM Tooling map section

You have the constraint. You have a focused map. Now: **what's your move?**

## Types of Moves

### Natural Evolution (Dashed Arrow)
Market forces, you don't control it
- Code → AI-generated code
- Servers → Cloud

### Intended Move (Solid Double Arrow)
You actively push
- Open-source your tool → accelerate commoditization
- Document domain knowledge → make it accessible
- Automate architecture checks → industrialize decisions

### Future State (Red)
Where you want to end up
- Domain knowledge: (heads) Custom → (documented) Product
- Code review: (manual) Custom → (AI-assisted) Product

## Your Task

### Step 1: Pick Your Move (5 min)

**For Harbour Insurance, common moves:**

**Evolve Code Review:**
- From: Manual senior review
- To: Distributed + AI-assisted
- How: Exploit (small PRs, ensemble), then elevate (train reviewers)

**Industrialize Architecture:**
- From: Architect bottleneck
- To: Architecture as code, automated checks
- How: Document as executable rules

**Commoditize Domain Knowledge:**
- From: Knowledge in heads (inertia)
- To: Curated, searchable
- How: Stewardship + AI curation

**Draw it on your map.**

### Step 2: Draw the Move (3 min)

1. Mark **current state**
2. Draw **arrow** (solid double = intended)
3. Mark **target state** (red or "TARGET")
4. Note **timeline** if relevant

### Step 3: Pick a Gameplay (4 min)

**Common patterns:**
- **Education:** Train more people → reduce expert dependency
- **Constraint Exploitation:** Use scarcity to force prioritization
- **Co-evolution:** Evolve review AND knowledge together
- **Open Approaches:** Document and share → accelerate standard

**Write your gameplay on the map.**

### Step 4: Who Loses? (3 min)

Every evolution creates winners and losers.

| Move | Who Loses | Why They Resist |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| Evolve review | Senior devs | Lose gatekeeping status |
| Industrialize architecture | Architect | Loses control |
| Commoditize knowledge | Domain experts | Expertise becomes less scarce |

**Note who loses.** That's your inertia prediction.

## Discussion

1. What's your move (from → to)?
2. What's your gameplay?
3. Who loses when it succeeds?
4. **What change in YOUR organization could you map Monday?**
