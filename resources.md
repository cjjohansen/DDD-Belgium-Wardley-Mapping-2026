# Wardley Mapping Resources

## Essential Reading

### The Foundation

**Wardley Maps: Topographical Intelligence in Business**  
Simon Wardley | 2016-2018 (living updates)

The canonical text. 19 chapters covering needs → maps → climate → doctrine → gameplay.

**Free** (CC BY-SA 4.0):
- [Medium](https://medium.com/wardleymaps)
- [PDF](https://www.swardleymaps.com/docs/wardley-maps-book.pdf)
- [Audiobook](https://feststelltaste.github.io/wardley-maps-audiobook/)
