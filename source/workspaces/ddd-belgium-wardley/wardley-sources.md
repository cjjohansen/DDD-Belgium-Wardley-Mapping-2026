## 3. Core concept reference (canonical list + best free URL each)

### The frame: strategy cycle and five factors
Sun Tzu's factors (purpose, landscape, climate, doctrine, leadership) run as a cycle (via Boyd's
OODA). Best single free URL: **https://learnwardleymapping.com/introduction** (definitions of
Wardley Mapping, Wardley Map, strategy cycle) — narrative version in Book Ch1
(https://www.swardleymaps.com/docs/wardley-maps-book.pdf, or Medium
https://medium.com/wardleymaps/on-being-lost-2ef5f05eb1ec).

### The axes and map construction (cup of tea)
- **Best single free URL:** https://swardley.medium.com/a-good-enough-map-eaed8a525bf4 — tea shop:
  users → needs → chain → visibility ordering → evolution placement → questions you can now ask.
- Step-by-step with a bigger example: https://www.cio.com/article/196094/an-introduction-to-wardley-value-chain-mapping.html
- Canonical reference definition (value chain vs graph; four stages Genesis → Custom → Product(+Rental) → Commodity(+Utility)): https://learnwardleymapping.com/landscape/
- Short classroom version: https://www.wardleymaps.com/guides/wardley-mapping-101

### Climatic patterns (the "rules of the game" you cannot stop, only anticipate)
- **Best single free URL (full table):** https://learnwardleymapping.com/climate — patterns grouped
  as **Competitors** (e.g. "competitors' actions will change the game", "most competitors have poor
  situational awareness"), **Components** ("everything evolves through supply and demand
  competition", "no choice over evolution", "characteristics change as components evolve",
  "components can co-evolve"), **Financial** ("higher order systems create new sources of value",
  "future value is inversely proportional to the certainty we have over it", "capital flows to new
  areas of value", "creative destruction"), **Inertia** ("success breeds inertia", "inertia can kill
  an organisation"), **Prediction** ("the less evolved something is the more uncertain it is",
  "economy has cycles", "two different forms of disruption", "a war (point of industrialisation)
  causes organisations to evolve"), **Speed** ("efficiency enables innovation", "shifts from product
  to utility tend to demonstrate a punctuated equilibrium").
- Narrative teaching version: Book Ch3 *Exploring the map* —
  https://medium.com/wardleymaps/exploring-the-map-ad0266fad59b (patterns you cannot stop but can
  anticipate; includes "an exercise for the reader").
- Deeper patterns (co-evolution, Peace-War-Wonder): Book Ch9 —
  https://medium.com/wardleymaps/anticipation-89692e9b0ced.

### Doctrine (the 4 phases)
Doctrine = universal principles, applied regardless of landscape. Wardley's doctrine is taught in
**four implementation phases** (stop self-harm first):
- **Best single free URL:** https://learnwardleymapping.com/doctrine —
  - **Phase 1 — Stop Self-Destructive Behaviour:** common language; challenge assumptions; situational
    awareness; know your users; focus on user needs; remove bias & duplication; use appropriate
    methods; systematic learning; think small.
  - **Phase 2 — Becoming More Context Aware:** be transparent; outcome not contract; be pragmatic;
    use appropriate tools (e.g. mapping); think F.I.R.E.; use standards; move fast ("an imperfect
    plan executed today is better than a perfect plan executed tomorrow"); strategy is iterative;
    bias towards action; manage failure; manage inertia; effectiveness over efficiency; aptitude &
    attitude; small teams; distribute power.
  - **Phase 3 — Better for Less:** be the owner; think big; strategy is complex; commit to direction,
    be adaptive along the path ("crossing the river by feeling the stones"); be humble; bias to the
    new; optimise flow; do better with less; set exceptional standards; seek the best; provide
    purpose, mastery & autonomy.
  - **Phase 4 — Continuously Evolving:** exploit the landscape; there is no core; listen to your
    ecosystems; design for constant evolution; there is no one culture (Pioneers, Settlers, Town
    Planners). (Table adapted by Tasshin Fogleman from Wardley's posts + *Better for Less*.)
- Teaching essay: https://learnwardleymapping.com/2020/12/07/getting-started-with-wardleys-doctrine-is-easier-than-you-think/
- Narrative version: Book Ch4 — https://medium.com/wardleymaps/doctrine-8bb0015688e5.

### Game plays (context-specific stratagems)
- **Best single free URL (categorised table):** https://learnwardleymapping.com/leadership —
  stratagems grouped as Accelerators (open approaches, co-operation, network effects, market
  enablement), De-accelerators (constraint exploitation, IPR), Dealing with toxicity, Ecosystem
  (ILC sensing engines, two-factor markets, tower & moat), User Perception (FUD, bundling, education),
  Attacking (centre of gravity, fool's mate, press-release process), Competitor (ambush, sapping,
  talent raid), Defensive (raise barriers, threat acquisition), Markets (last man standing, standards
  game), Poison, Positional (fast follower, first mover, land grab). Includes the worked "increase
  competition in content" scenario.
- Source catalogue: https://blog.gardeviance.org/2015/05/on-61-different-forms-of-gameplay.html
- Worked play: https://blog.gardeviance.org/2014/11/fools-mate-in-business.html
- Compact definitions: https://www.wardleymaps.com/glossary/gameplay

## 4. Workshop-ready exercises and formats

1. **How to run a Wardley Map workshop (Pip Decks guide, by Charles Burdett)** —
   https://guides.pipdecks.com/workshop-tactics/how-to-run-a-wardley-map-workshop
   Concrete session recipe: split into groups so you get *varied maps to compare*; use whiteboard,
   the Miro Wardley Map template, or OnlineWardleyMaps. Encapsulates the workshop ethos: "The act of
   creating the map is as important as the map itself… like excavating dinosaur bones." Also covers
   scoping ("the more you limit the scope, the more useful") and reading/prioritising with maps.
   (Also linked from Wardley's own resources page.)

2. **Wardley Mapping Facilitation (Ben Mosior)** —
   https://learnwardleymapping.com/2021/03/15/wardley-mapping-facilitation/
   How to actually run the room: name the two obstacles (legitimacy — "is this worth it?"; literacy —
   "how does it work?"), then just get sticky notes (Miro) and follow the book's Ch2 sequence: start
   with users, work down. Companion posts: *Pragmatic Facilitation* (workshop reality vs concept) and
   *Getting Stuck Is Problem #1* (free-word brainstorm warm-up when the group stalls).

3. **Simon Wardley's one-day workshop agenda (Harvard Kennedy School / digital HKS, Belfer Center)** —
   https://www.belfercenter.org/event/wardley-maps-workshop-hosted-digital-hks
   A published full-day structure straight from the inventor: introduction to strategy → scenario
   (case study) analysis → learning to (Wardley) map → scenario revisited → discussion. Steal the
   arc: *meet a scenario, fail at it, learn mapping, re-attack the scenario*.

4. **The book's built-in exercises (Ch3, Ch4, Ch5, Ch6, Ch8 each end with "an exercise for the
   reader"; Ch12–13 are a complete case exercise)** — https://www.swardleymaps.com/docs/wardley-maps-book.pdf
   Ch12 *The Scenario* hands participants a fictional company described department by department
   (marketing, sales, digital, strategy, finance) with an explicit task; Ch13 shows the map and
   analysis. This is a ready-made multi-stakeholder case you can re-skin for a DDD domain.

5. **Three starter exercises + the two-tea-maps debate (wardleymaps.com, Krzysztof Daniel)** —
   exercises at https://www.wardleymaps.com/faqs/how-to-start-wardley-mapping (map your morning
   coffee; map a simple app; map your job) and the team-introduction format at
   https://www.wardleymaps.com/guides/introducing-wardley-mapping-to-your-team ("Pick one real
   upcoming decision"; show two near-identical tea maps with different strategy and let the room
   argue; "Don't lead with the label").

6. **Six-rung teaching path & paired before/after maps (wardley-maps.sgit.ai)** —
   https://wardley-maps.sgit.ai/start/index.html
   A ladder from "I've read the intro" to "I can argue about a placement". Steal two techniques:
   **paired before/after maps** ("draw it twice — the distance between the two is the case for the
   service") and "a map is a claim, not a picture — say what would falsify it" (a great discussion
   rule for a DDD crowd used to defending design decisions).

7. **More formats & materials:** Map Camp returns as a full-day **MAP CAMP Workshop, 12 Nov 2026,
   London** (fundamentals + case studies + a fictional "magical system detection" scenario +
   interactive Q&A) — history and format at https://wardley-maps.sgit.ai/resources/map-camp.html .
   Ben Mosior & David Holl's **Strategy Tactics** card deck (Pip Decks) is a purchasable exercise
   deck. Tricia Bagley's Coursera guided project "User Strategy Development with Wardley Maps in
   Miro" is a hands-on remote-workshop format (listed, with templates, in the community index
   https://github.com/wardley-maps-community/awesome-wardley-maps — CC0). Free drawing templates
   (PowerPoint/Google Slides) at https://learnwardleymapping.com/portfolio/powerpoint and
   /portfolio/google-slides.
